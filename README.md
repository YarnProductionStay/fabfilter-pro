The software offers a range of capabilities including equalization, compression, reverb, and other effects that allow users to manipulate audio with precision. Users can expect a versatile toolkit that integrates seamlessly into their digital audio workstation (DAW), facilitating a productive workflow and delivering high-quality audio outputs.
